Privacy Policy

Zooliwood Studio · last updated October 9, 2026

What data the tool accesses

With the explicit authorization of the account owner (OAuth), the tool accesses only the Zooliwood team's own accounts: basic profile information (display name, avatar, profile links), account statistics (followers, likes, video count), the list of our own public videos and their statistics, and the permission to upload a video as a draft that the owner finishes and publishes manually in the platform's app.

How the data is used

The data is used only to prepare our own videos and to follow the performance of our own channels. It is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Where the data is stored

Access tokens and statistics are stored locally on the Zooliwood team's own computer, in files readable only by the owner. They are not stored on any shared server.

Retention and deletion

Statistics are kept only as long as useful for our own analysis. Access can be revoked at any time from the platform's settings (connected apps), and local tokens are then deleted.

Third-party users

The tool is not offered to other users and collects no data about other people.
